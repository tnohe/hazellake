Approach

One Business. Long-Term Ownership.

Hazel Lake Partners was founded with a single goal: to acquire, operate, and build one great business in the Midwest for the long term.

We are looking for one great business to own and operate for the long term. We understand that every owner has different goals when considering a transition, and we are flexible about what that transition looks like. Our objective is to build on what has made the business successful while investing in its people and future growth.

01

Flexible Transition

Working with owners to structure a transition around their goals and the needs of the business.

02

Stewardship

Respecting what has been built while investing thoughtfully in the company's continued success.

03

Long-Term Ownership

Building for the long term, without a predetermined investment horizon or exit.

Investment Profile

What We Look For

Hazel Lake Partners focuses on established, profitable businesses in fragmented industries with durable demand and attractive long-term fundamentals. We are particularly drawn to essential products and services, recurring customer needs, and businesses that benefit from strong local or regional market positions.

Target Industries

  • Essential & Field Services
  • Logistics & Transportation
  • Healthcare Services
  • Distribution & Wholesaling
  • Light Manufacturing & Fabrication
  • Agriculture & Ag-Tech

Financial Profile

Philosophy

A Different Approach to Ownership

Hazel Lake Partners is not building a portfolio of companies. We are looking for one great business where we can invest our time, energy, and capital alongside the people who made it successful.

We believe great businesses are built over decades, not investment cycles. Our goal is to preserve what works, invest thoughtfully in future growth, and create lasting value for employees, customers, partners, and the community.

Our goal is to honor what an owner has built and carry that legacy forward.

The Name

Hazel Lake

Hazel is a color without a single definition. A lake has no marked path. Together, the name reflects the judgment, perspective, and conviction required to choose a direction and see it through.
